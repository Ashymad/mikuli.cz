So I shall write: "My Wife,  
What would it be, my life?  
If you were not around  
or you have never found,  
a corner in your heart,  
for me to take a part  
in, settle there for good.  
I wonder if it would  
feel like there is twofold  
beauty and magic in this world  
when sunrise paints the sky  
pink and reminds me why  
I keep my head on high  
and power through, just to  
see tomorrow with You."  
Signed, with love, your baboo
